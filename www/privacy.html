Privacy Policy — RUSH HOUR

Last updated: July 16, 2026

RUSH HOUR (“the Game”) is an endless runner entertainment application. This policy describes how the Game handles information.

Data we store

The Game stores high scores, best distance, coin totals, run count, and mute preference only on your device using browser or app local storage. This data never leaves your device in the base version of the Game.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or login
  • No advertising identifiers in the base build
  • No analytics or crash reporting in the base build
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is suitable for a general audience as a skill-based arcade runner.

Changes

If future versions add ads, analytics, or online features, this policy will be updated and store listings will be revised accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher email listed on the store listing page.
