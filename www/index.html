How to Play

  • Run — auto-sprint through a 3D daylight city.
  • Lanes — ← → or swipe to dodge.
  • Jump / Slide — clear crates & low signs.
  • Oncoming cars — traffic comes at you the other way. Change lanes to dodge. Low cars can be jumped; SUVs and pickups cannot.
  • Coins — go to your wallet after each run.
  • Store — buy & equip new runners with bonuses.
  • Progress — wallet, best distance & unlocks save on this device.
  • Cop — only catches you if you bump an obstacle or car. Jump crates, slide under signs, dodge oncoming traffic.
